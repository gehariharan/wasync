Welcome to wasync

This installs wasync, a local WhatsApp archive with a terminal app, a command-line tool and a local engine, into /usr/local/bin.

The next step lists the parts you can install. wasync is always installed. AXI for agents (whatsapp-axi) is optional and is off unless you tick it. Choose it if you want AI agents such as Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode to work with your archive.

Your existing archive and pairing are never touched by this installer.
