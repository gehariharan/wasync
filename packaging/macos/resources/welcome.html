Welcome to wasync

This installs wasync, a local WhatsApp archive with a terminal app, a command-line tool and a local engine, into /usr/local/bin.

The next step lists what is installed. wasync and AXI for agents (wasync-axi, the command AI agents such as Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode use to work with your archive) are always installed together. This installer does not connect or change any agent; you connect agents later from wasync's Connect an agent step.

Your existing archive and pairing are never touched by this installer.
